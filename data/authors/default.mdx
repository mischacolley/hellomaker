Hi 👋🏻 I'm **Mischa Colley**. For more than 15 years I've been <a href="https://opencampusproject.com" target="_blank">casually exploring</a> the evolving landscape of education, schooling and learning through an alternative lens. HelloMaker is one of the projects that has come out of that exploration.

In the context of HelloMaker I like to introduce myself by saying I'm first and foremost an unschool dad to two fantastic young people who are 8 and 10.

With this project I get an excuse to explore and tinker with tech in a playful way that I hope will keep my interest in it alive longer than my work as a <a href="https://mischacolley.com.au/" target="_blank">Creative Technologist</a> otherwise might.
